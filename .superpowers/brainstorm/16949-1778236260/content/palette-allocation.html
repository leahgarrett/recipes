The principle

Saturation lives in the food photos. Everything in the chrome — surfaces, type, even the buttons — sits at low chroma so the photos pop. The brown CTA reads as "the interactive thing" because it's the warmest, deepest color on the page, not because it's bright.

Does this allocation work for you? Or would you like a brighter accent (and accept that it'll be more visually present near photos)?
