Brown Butter Pancakes
BREAKFAST
Toasted, nutty, the kind of stack you slow down for.
⏱ Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min
Servings
4
Ingredients
1 cup all-purpose flour
2 tbsp brown butter, cooled
1 large egg
¾ cup buttermilk
Instructions
1
PREP
Brown the butter in a small saucepan until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn golden. Set aside to cool.
2
MIX
Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. In a separate bowl, whisk egg, buttermilk, and the cooled brown butter.
3
COOK
Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-low. Pour ¼-cup pools of batter and cook until bubbles form across the surface, about 2 minutes. Flip and cook 1 minute more.
